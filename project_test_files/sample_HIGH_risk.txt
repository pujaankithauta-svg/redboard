REDBOARD DECISION BRIEF — AI CLAIMS PROCESSING ASSISTANT
=========================================================
Project: ClaimsIQ v1.0
Date: June 2026
Submitted by: ML Platform Team, Ogha Inc


PROPOSAL
--------
Deploy a multi-tenant AI-powered insurance claims processing assistant to production.

The system automatically triages incoming insurance claims submitted via API and web portal,
extracts structured information from uploaded documents (PDFs, TIFF images, medical reports,
police reports, contractor quotes), assesses claim validity against our proprietary rule engine,
and recommends one of three dispositions to human adjusters: APPROVE, REJECT, or ESCALATE.

Scope: 50,000 monthly active users across 12 enterprise insurance company clients.
Volume: Target 10,000 claims/day at launch, scaling to 100,000 within 6 months.
Decision weight: AI recommendation is shown to adjuster before they make final decision.
Automation rate: 40% of routine claims auto-approved without adjuster review if confidence > 95%.


CONTEXT AND TECHNICAL DETAILS
------------------------------
Model Architecture:
  - Stage 1: Document OCR via AWS Textract + custom post-processing
  - Stage 2: Information extraction using GPT-4o (Azure OpenAI) via LangChain
  - Stage 3: Rule-based validation engine v2.1 (proprietary, 847 rules)
  - Stage 4: Risk scoring via XGBoost classifier (our own model)
  - Stage 5: Final recommendation via ensemble of Stage 3 + Stage 4 outputs

Training Data:
  - 2.3 million historical claims from 2019 to 2024
  - Source: US market only — auto insurance (60%), homeowner insurance (35%), other (5%)
  - Date range: 5 years, but COVID period (2020-2021) has known anomalies we have not filtered
  - Label quality: Human adjuster decisions used as ground truth. Known adjuster inconsistency
    rate of approximately 12% on borderline cases (internal audit finding from 2023)

Evaluation Results (internal testing):
  - Overall accuracy: 91.2% on held-out test set of 5,000 claims
  - Precision on APPROVE: 94.1%
  - Recall on APPROVE: 89.3%
  - Precision on REJECT: 88.7%
  - Recall on REJECT: 92.1%
  - Fraud detection recall: 88% (12% of fraudulent claims not flagged)
  - False positive rate on REJECT: 6% (legitimate claims incorrectly rejected)
  - Test set composition: Same distribution as training data — NOT independently sampled

What we have NOT tested:
  - Health insurance claims
  - Life insurance claims
  - Commercial property claims above $500K
  - Non-English documents (Spanish, Mandarin — 8% of incoming volume)
  - Claims involving litigation or legal disputes
  - Catastrophic weather events (CAT claims) with surge volume
  - Claims from new client verticals we are onboarding in Q4

Deployment Environment:
  - Multi-tenant SaaS on AWS ECS (Fargate)
  - Each client gets isolated data store (separate RDS instances)
  - Latency target: Under 3 seconds per claim for standard processing
  - Availability SLA: 99.5% uptime

PII and Data Handling:
  - Claims contain: SSNs, DOBs, bank account details, medical diagnoses, vehicle VINs,
    property addresses, witness statements, legal correspondence
  - Field-level encryption at rest using AWS KMS
  - No HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) in place yet with any client
  - GDPR assessment: Not completed. Two clients have EU policyholders.


OBJECTIVES
----------
Primary KPIs:
  - Reduce average claim processing time from 8 days to under 24 hours
  - Reduce cost per claim processed by 35%
  - Achieve adjuster satisfaction score above 4.2/5.0
  - Maintain or improve claim settlement accuracy vs baseline

Secondary KPIs:
  - 95% adjuster adoption rate within 90 days of launch
  - Zero Tier-1 incidents (customer-facing outages) in first 90 days
  - Fraud detection rate improvement from current 76% to 88%


KNOWN RISKS (AS IDENTIFIED BY TEAM)
------------------------------------
1. Model accuracy drops on edge cases — mitigation: route low confidence scores to manual review
2. Non-English documents — mitigation: flag for human review, planned language model upgrade Q4
3. Data drift as new claim types are added — mitigation: monthly model retraining planned
4. Adjuster over-reliance on AI recommendation — mitigation: UI designed to show recommendation
   only after adjuster reviews claim summary (NOT before)

HUMAN OVERSIGHT PLAN
--------------------
- All REJECT recommendations require human adjuster confirmation before execution
- APPROVE recommendations with confidence below 95% require human review
- Auto-approval only for: standard auto claims under $5,000, confidence above 95%, no fraud flags
- Full audit trail of all AI recommendations and human decisions stored for 7 years
- Adjuster can override AI recommendation at any time with required reason code


ROLLBACK PLAN
-------------
- Feature flag per client to disable AI recommendations
- Shadow mode capability (AI runs but recommendations not shown)
- Rollback to manual processing within 2 hours if P0 incident declared
- No automated rollback trigger currently defined


SUCCESS METRICS (POST-LAUNCH MONITORING)
-----------------------------------------
- Daily accuracy vs adjuster override rate
- Claims per hour per adjuster (productivity metric)
- Customer complaint rate related to AI decisions
- Fraud claims identified vs missed (reviewed monthly)
- Model confidence distribution (drift detection)
- Latency p50, p95, p99
- Error rate by claim type and client
