What is Redboard?

An adversarial AI review panel that forces your shipping decisions through structured, parallel expert scrutiny — before you ship.

🏛️ The Problem

Investment committees, clinical trial boards, and supreme courts all use adversarial review by design. AI shipping decisions do not. Teams argue in Slack, someone senior wins, and the wrong call ships. Redboard is the fix.

⚔️ The Approach

Five specialist agents attack your proposal in parallel — Safety, Business, Data Quality, Compliance, and Contrarian. A sixth synthesizes their findings into a verdict with minority dissent preserved.

📋 The Output

A structured decision memo with verdict (SHIP / DO NOT SHIP / SHIP WITH CONDITIONS), confidence level, risk summary, key findings, conditions, and next steps. Ready for leadership in PDF.

🔒 The Security

JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, security headers (CSP, HSTS, XSS protection), input sanitization, per-user data isolation, full audit logging, and consent management.

☁️ Built on Google Cloud

Gemini 2.5 Flash for all agents, Google ADK for multi-agent orchestration, deployable on Cloud Run or GKE, with Vertex AI model access and Google Cloud credentials.

🎯 Who it's for

AI/ML teams at companies deploying production AI features. Data scientists who need a structured pre-mortem. Engineering leaders who want an audit trail for AI decisions.

Technology stack
